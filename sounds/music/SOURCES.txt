Simple Wars — Music sources & attributions
==========================================

Playlist inspired by Hearts of Iron (2002) classical BGM.
Only commercially usable recordings are shipped (public domain,
U.S. government works, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA).

Attribution-required tracks are marked ATTR below.
Compositions by deceased composers are public domain unless noted.
