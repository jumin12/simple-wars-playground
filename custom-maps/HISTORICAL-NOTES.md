# Historical scenario notes

These are historically inspired RTS missions. Terrain, distances, army sizes, chronology and unit roles are adapted to Simple Wars. Tactical outcomes are player-driven. The game has no aircraft or minefield simulation: Midway abstracts air groups with its existing unit archetypes, and El Alamein represents defensive belts with forts. Normandy landing infantry uses marine rules to model landing craft; ordinary armor begins on supplied dry land.

- **Thermopylae: The Hot Gates — 480 BCE.** The coastal pass, nearby mountains and a flanking trail inform the map. The counterattack objective is an alternative game outcome. [Herodotus on Thermopylae, translated extracts](https://www.livius.org/sources/content/herodotus/herodotus-on-thermopylae/).
- **Hastings: Senlac Ridge — 14 October 1066.** Norman attacks against the English high-ground position inform the ridge and cavalry approaches. [English Heritage battlefield history](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/1066-battle-of-hastings-abbey-and-battlefield/history-and-stories/what-happened-battle-hastings/).
- **Austerlitz: The Pratzen Heights — 2 December 1805.** The Goldbach crossings, central heights and allied southern movement inform the three-lane design. [French Ministry of the Armed Forces](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/terre/mieux-nous-connaitre/histoire-larmee-terre/larmee-terre-travers-ses-batailles/2-decembre-1805-bataille), [Fondation Napoléon tactical account](https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/articles/the-battle-of-austerlitz-and-the-principles-of-war/).
- **El Alamein: Breakthrough — opening on 23 October 1942.** The Mediterranean coast, southern depression and defended desert frontage inform the map. [National Army Museum account](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/battle-alamein).

Existing D-Day, Kursk and Midway missions retain their purchased IDs, prices and completion records. D-Day has fewer crowded town objectives and spaced batteries; Kursk separates nearby town footprints; Midway has distinct playable islands and a wider naval preview. Every map begins with empty town buildings, consistent with the general match rules. Opening force reduction still applies. All missions remain retryable until a victory consumes their one-shot entitlement.
